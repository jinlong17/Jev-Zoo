A sourced catalog. An evidenced platform.

JevZoo is an independent community project developing resource discovery and executable training/evaluation together.

Inclusion and evidence

Curated resources directly help model use, training, evaluation and representative applications. The broad directory retains useful engineering and applications; invalid or unresolved sources stay in a review lane. Canonical duplicates are merged without deleting discovery evidence or reasons.

Reachable links, primary capability claims, visible loaders, real smoke and full evaluations are different evidence. Free access, open code, open weights, public data and hosted services are recorded separately.
